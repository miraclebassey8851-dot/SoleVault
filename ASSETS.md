# Asset notes

- Local image files are stored in `assets/`. Confirm their provenance and redistribution rights before commercial resale.
- Product catalogue images are loaded from Unsplash URLs in `store.js`; they require internet access. Check the applicable image licence and the marketplace's rules before bundling or reselling any image.
- Google Fonts (Inter and Zen Dots) and Phosphor Icons are loaded externally by the pages. Internet access is required for those resources.
